How a game works

  1. Choose a topic. Spin the wheel 5 times for subtopics (3 spins with 1–2 players).
  2. Everyone votes on one subtopic to play.
  3. Ten rounds. Round 1 needs 1 right answer, round 2 needs 2, and so on up to 10.
  4. Every right answer inflates your head. Hit the round's target and it pops: you're through. Three misses in a round and you're out.

Picking a topic here starts a game on this device, passed around. Choose Play online to give everyone their own device. Online, all players answer the same question at once.
